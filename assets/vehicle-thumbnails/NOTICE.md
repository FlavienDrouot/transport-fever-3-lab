These purchase-list miniatures are original Transport Fever 3 artwork by Urban Games.
They are separate from this project's MIT-licensed code.

The PNGs preserve the installed TGA images' decoded pixels, alpha and original dimensions.
The private extraction package and its full manifest are not included in the website.
`data/vehicle-thumbnails.json` retains source identities, dimensions and PNG checksums.
Formation previews reuse these images in order; mirroring is applied only at display time.
